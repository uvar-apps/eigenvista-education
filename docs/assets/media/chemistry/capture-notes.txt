EigenVista chemistry media

The workflow PNG and CSV downloads were exported by the Apple Silicon
EigenVista 0.10.6 desktop release through its bundled connector. The GUI
captures and controller replays show the upstream EigenVista production
browser build at 1520 × 900 in Chromium. The browser replays use the same
application controller as the desktop connector. Capture sources and the
scene plans are in tools/media-input/chemistry of the education repository.

Verified outputs

1AON ADP author chain A residue 1 is label chain W. The 4 Å queries give
27 seeds, 74 matching atom centers, and 154 atoms in the full-residue
neighborhood. The monitor uses source indices 657 and 58677 for THR A:91
OG1 and ADP A:1 O2B. Its measured distance is 2.54551095 Å.

The two 1CRN cartoons use the same fitted camera with horizontal and vertical
orbits of 22° and −19°. The SES and SAS use the same source atoms, Bondi /
Mantina radii, 1.4 Å probe, and 0.5 Å spacing. The accepted meshes contain
27,608 and 34,724 triangles. The two images use the same camera and color.

The seven-atom TiO6 image shows the 1.950 Å equatorial and 2.100 Å axial
Ti–O distances. The CSV preserves those two distances, two angles and the
signed torsion in the supplied local geometry.
